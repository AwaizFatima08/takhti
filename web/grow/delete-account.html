Delete your Takhti Grow account

Takhti Grow is published by HomiLabs on Google Play. You can delete your account and all its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Takhti Grow and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.
  3. Confirm your password, or choose your Google account again.

Your data is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email homilabs.smc@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Takhti Grow account". We will delete your account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, reminder times, time zone, age and terms confirmation
  • Your lesson progress, streak and quiz answers
  • All your reflections
  • Feedback you sent
  • Your sign-in account

Nothing is kept, except crash reports (technical data with no reflections), which Firebase Crashlytics deletes automatically within 90 days, and short-lived Firebase backups.
