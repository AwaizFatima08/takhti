## Full description
Takhti Grow turns Takhti's neuroscience videos into one small lesson a day.

Every lesson follows the same five-minute loop:
• Read — the key idea in three short points
• Watch — a short clip from Takhti on YouTube
• Do — one small action to try in your day
• Reflect — in the evening, note how it went
• Check — an optional three-question quiz

Your streak, progress and reflections are saved to your account, so they move with you to a new phone. Two gentle daily reminders help you keep the habit; you choose the times or turn them off.

PILOT — 4 LESSONS. This first version is a pilot with four lessons. Tell us what to improve from the feedback screen at the end.

Language: the app is in English. The video clips are in Urdu (English captions where available).

For adults (18+). Content from Takhti Online (Adeel Imtiaz), used with permission. App by HomiLabs.

Takhti Grow shares educational content about learning and habits. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
